# FlowGPT

> FlowGPT is a community platform for discovering, sharing, and chatting with AI prompts, characters, and chatbots. Backed by DCM Ventures, it operates as a ChatGPT prompt and AI-character marketplace where creators publish prompts, roleplay characters, and AI apps, and users engage them through free chat, leaderboards, bounties, and paid membership tiers. FlowGPT does not currently publish a public developer REST API, OpenAPI definition, or first-party SDK; this file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the FlowGPT company profile.
